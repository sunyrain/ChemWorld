## Additional analysis of retained predictions and core histories

### Do final predictions retain matched-world differences?

This post hoc analysis uses all thirty matched-world sessions and 1,080 retained predictions. It compares B minus A for each parameter pair, query and response after averaging three sessions within each world. Each reference is the same five-observation mean used in original scoring. All 600 actual reference action sequences match the twelve common query definitions. All 180 pair-query-response contrasts are retained, including small gaps and wrong directions. The supplementary display of gaps at least three assay standard deviations uses the existing physical scale; this is a post hoc descriptive threshold, not a test of statistical significance. Repeated target concentrations and common worlds preclude treating these as independent trials.

The forecasts retain substantial world differences. This does not establish unique mechanism identification or isolate a gain caused by research: the block has no NoResearch arm, and starter observations are close but not identical. Prediction sensitivity and explanation content are separate outcomes. Full contrasts, query actions and original session forecasts are included in the portable archive.

### Internal scientific review of seven core histories

This is a further internal review of public records, not independent human coding or a blinded second rating. The reviewer has seen the manuscript and outcomes. Notes, committed operations, measurements and later accounts are checked separately. Exact quotations and action timing checks support the identified sequence; scientific interpretation retains the boundaries below. The public source packet allows another researcher to inspect the history without relying on the manuscript paraphrase. It does not estimate prevalence.

### Levels of support

| Evidence | Coverage | What it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrated design | 450 planned sessions in four distinct matrices | Scope of research conditions; not 450 replications of a process |
| Controlled contrasts | Fixed worlds, condition cells and retained failures | Condition-specific operation and prediction differences |
| Complete record reading | 300 new sessions | Coverage of internal review; not prevalence of every selected phenomenon |
| Core histories | Five new and two matched-world, selected post hoc | Actual temporally located transitions and their evidential scope |
| Paired-world forecasts | 30 sessions; 180 correlated contrasts | Whether final predictions reflect the world-specific response differences |
