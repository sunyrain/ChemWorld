# Appendix F. Complete most-dilute equilibrium predictions

## F.1 Original intervals and campaign-specific observations

The main-text equilibrium comparison shows point predictions for the most dilute recipe. Table F1 retains all fifteen original 80% prediction intervals and each campaign's source-response range. These are prediction intervals issued by the agent, not confidence intervals on an estimated mean. Each source range covers twelve final assays; the reference is the mean of the original five evaluator observations and was unavailable during prediction or retrospective reflection.
