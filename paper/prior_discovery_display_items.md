# Display plan — priors, evidence correction and unseen action

The main text contains only completed evidence that answers the three central questions. Early
exploratory cohorts, the one-world parametric pilot, incomplete prototype, repair trajectory and
operational diagnostics are Supplementary Information and are not used to extend the main-text
denominators.

## Figure 1 — Capability chain

- **Question:** Why is endpoint success insufficient?
- **Panels:** initial-model intervention; repeated search and measurement; evaluator-owned held-out
  truth; conversion losses from prediction to law and action.
- **Main-text role:** conceptual scaffold; no outcome claim.
- **Source:** `paper/figures/prior-discovery/figure-1-prior-to-law.pdf`.

## Figure 2 — Completed evidence architecture

- **Question:** How are the three completed evidence blocks separated?
- **Panels:** matched task--world clusters; opaque/aligned/misspecified arms; locus-specific validation;
  prospective search, matched evidence, evaluator scoring and open-action assay.
- **Main-text role:** design and denominator map.
- **Source:** `paper/figures/prior-discovery/figure-2-formal-cohort.pdf`.

## Figure 3 — Prediction, law and action dissociation

- **Question:** Does evidence repair the wrong prior and become a reusable regularity?
- **Panels:** selective-correction contrast, prediction improvement, executable-law error and blind
  incumbent replay.
- **Main-text role:** prospective cohort and valid matched-evidence results.
- **Key numbers:** 135/135 sessions; 1,243/1,260 experiments; selective-correction gates
  $p=0.990$, $0.079$ and $1.000$; law better/equal/worse 50/1/84; blind replay better/equal/worse
  1/119/1.
- **Source:** current evaluator capability-chain figure, copied at build time to the reader-facing
  filename `figure-3-capability-chain.pdf`.

## Figure 4 — Unseen-action selection

- **Question:** Can learned information support an unseen action?
- **Panels:** selected rank, normalized regret, joint law--action categories and task-level means.
- **Main-text role:** formal multi-task open-action assay.
- **Key numbers:** 45/45 scheduled cell records; 42 eligible readouts; 240/240 truth and exact
  replay; 11/42 Top-1; 0/42 adequate-law/correct-action.
- **Source:** `paper/figures/prior-discovery/figure-6-open-action-formal.pdf`.
