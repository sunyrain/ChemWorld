## Development denominators retained in the draft

- WellAU/Codex: 45 terminal cells, 44 completed cells and 176/180 complete experiments.
- DeepSeek recovery-amended: 45 terminal cells, 43 completed cells, 174/180 complete experiments
  and 45/45 exact replay.
- DeepSeek five-task development completion: 75/75 terminal cells, 69/75 completed/qualified cells,
  290/300 complete experiments, 69 recovered MCP failures, 0 provider errors, 3 resource rejections
  and 75/75 exact replay. The immutable seed-0 failures for partition discovery and safety-constrained
  reaction are retained while seeds 1--4 are added by a separate continuation; the five-task closeout
  remains operational/descriptive and does not support formal prior inference, transfer or a
  cross-provider ranking.
- DeepSeek five-task evaluator confirmation: 100/100 registered truth queries and 414/414 blind
  replays completed with exact replay and zero provider calls; final checkpoint predictions were
  scoreable for 72/75 cells and final law summaries were executable for 71/75. The development H3
  mean is -0.042 (7/25 positive), and no recommendation beats the observed incumbent. These are
  descriptive post-hoc outcomes, not the public formal result.
- WellAU electrochemical parametric D1: 3/3 participant cells, 12/12 complete experiments and 12/12
  checkpoints; 4/4 shared truth queries and 18/18 paired blind replays completed with exact replay and
  zero evaluator provider calls. Misspecified reliability fell from 0.70 to 0.03 and held-out error
  from 0.4200 to 0.1985, but its best endpoint score was 0.2743 versus 0.6703 for opaque. This is a
  single-world parametric case study, not a general initial-world-model result.
- Provider groups remain separate in every scientific contrast.

## Current public denominators and completed evaluator integration

- Current public composite: 45 task-world clusters, 135/135 terminal participant cells and
  1,243/1,260 complete experiments after replacing the complete A-S crystallization block.
- A-E public: five tasks × five public worlds × three initial-model arms = 25 independent clusters,
  75 participant cells and 585/600 complete experiments under the eight-experiment pattern.
- A-E private: the same 25-cluster/75-cell/600-experiment denominator under a one-shot sealed
  within-family cohort; this is replication, not compositional transfer.
- A-P completed 299/300 experiments across 30/30 terminal cells; A-S completed 359/360 across
  30/30 terminal cells. The two loci keep separate estimands and denominators.
- Participant collection contains 675/675 belief checkpoints, 6,300 registered query predictions
  and 24,300 query-metric values. The current-composite evaluator completed 420/420 truth executions,
  675/675 checkpoint scores, 135/135 law evaluations and 726/726 launched blind replays with zero
  provider calls. Four partition truth queries extrapolated beyond the participant search box but
  remained inside the physical runtime domain; they were evaluated without clipping.
- The v0.2 evaluator recovery forwarded the registered A-S world interventions into execution and
  exact replay from unit 1. A-E and A-P report blocks were unchanged; A-S law MAE became 0.1552 and
  the overall C2 decision remained false. The original v0.1 evaluator is historical only.
- Current matched-evidence evidence consists of 15 retained A-P sessions plus 15 A-S B2 sessions.
  B2 completed 80/80 provider-free truth queries, 30/30 provider turns and 360/360 scoring terms per
  stage; canary sessions are excluded.
