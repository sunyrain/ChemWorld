## Evidence roles that remain separate

- Work I qualifies its frozen bounded runtime and evidence release. A stale development-head Gate A
  does not invalidate that frozen release, and the release does not qualify every current Work II design.
- Earlier five-task development reached 75/75 terminal cells, 69/75 qualified, 290/300 complete
  experiments and 75/75 replay. These are protocol-development evidence, not part of the prospective
  denominator. The immutable seed-0 failures for partition discovery and safety-constrained reaction
  remain historical interface/qualification records, not paired scientific contrasts.
- The pre-recovery structural matched-evidence packet remains invalid for participant inference.
  Correcting evaluator truth after participants saw a defective packet cannot repair that cohort.
- DeepSeek-low B2 is a complete 15-session reasoning-budget comparison; its 20.9% output-token
  reduction is accounting, not a thinking-off or reasoning-superiority result. The A-P low partial
  has no qualified formal denominator.
- Original W2-50 longitudinal evidence and W2-64 law reanalysis remain DeepSeek-only.
  Dual-model C2, B3 and W2-61 are failure-aware matched descriptive surfaces, not a
  cross-provider leaderboard.
- W2-57/58/59 terminal canary failures and unstarted units remain in their original notes/reports.
  W2-61/62/63 are independent successors; they do not refill or overwrite those denominators.
- Observation-model screening, private confirmation and compositional transfer do not supply
  unreported positive participant outcomes. Within-family replication is not compositional transfer.

## New evidence needed

M0 and the independent M1 block are complete. The two-world canary remains development-only.
M1 did not establish a material benefit, and near-complete A/X agreement limits a general law-use
failure account. Cross-protocol differences are not randomized; arithmetic and law construction
are bundled in the L/F contrast. Zero-width observed bootstrap intervals are not equivalence proof.

raw evidence also helps and retrieval solves every candidate set. The next design should specify
source–target mechanism mappings, matched/mismatched knowledge, none/raw/nearest controls and matched
numerical tools, without selecting M3 failure cases. No new-condition block has run.
M2 is needed only for acquisition-effect claims; M4 addresses external validity.
