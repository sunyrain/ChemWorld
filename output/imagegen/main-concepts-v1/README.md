### Applicability synthesis

Source: the current article's final Results section. Aligned dilute-equilibrium predictions exemplify extension beyond an observed plateau. Crystallization purity exemplifies forecasts departing from a response that remains approximately stable under the tested interventions.

Caption draft: **Two complementary errors concern the applicability of observed relationships.** In the equilibrium example, the reference response leaves the measured plateau while forecasts remain near it. In crystallization, reference purity remains approximately stable while predictions are lower. Solid lines denote evaluator references and dashed lines denote agent forecasts. Curves and source markers are conceptual, without numerical scales; the crystallization horizontal direction does not imply an ordered intervention series or a measured monotonic trend. Process laws remain fixed. These patterns characterize predictions, not measured internal belief updates or universal failure frequencies.

Checked: forecast/reference line styles and labels; the direction of both contrasts; explicit schematic label; no numerical axes, confidence intervals or aggregate statistics generated by Imagegen.
