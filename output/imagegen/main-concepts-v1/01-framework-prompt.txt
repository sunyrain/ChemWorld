Use case: scientific-educational / infographic-diagram.
Create an English, publication-quality conceptual figure for a computational chemistry paper. This is Figure 1 artwork: how the original autonomous scientist experiments and is assessed. Landscape 3:2, high resolution. No overall title, no "Figure 1" text. Three well-composed panels with small bold lowercase a, b, c. White background, crisp flat vector-like scientific illustration, restrained teal #238B94 and slate #647887 accents, near-black #22272B body text, subtle pale-grey separators. Uniform normal-width Arial/Helvetica typography, generous readable labels without large empty zones. No gradients, shadows, glossy AI art, photoreal instruments, crystals, molecules as decoration, robots, or dense prose. A minimal black outline person-with-small-node glyph denotes the agent.

Panel a occupies the upper 58% of the canvas, heading "Autonomous research". A central legible closed-loop schematic: "Choose experiment" (agent beside a small recipe card), arrow to "Execute operations" (simple flat vessel, pipette and thermal-step icons), arrow to "Observe results" (small readout sheet), a clearly directed feedback arrow returning to Choose experiment. The return arrow label is "Next decision". A horizontal record strip under the loop contains three small document icons connected sequentially, label "Accumulated research history"; connect observations downward to it. Draw a clearly bounded, small grey enclosure behind/below the operation block labelled "Physical world"; inside only "Process laws", "Persistent state", "Finite resources". A small padlock beside "Private parameters" within that enclosure makes evaluator-only information distinct. No arrow from private parameters to the agent. A slim input above Choose experiment labelled "Public instructions + information". Show only modest scientific apparatus pictograms, not a laboratory scene.

Panel b occupies lower-left 32% width, heading "Matched conditions". Three aligned compact dossier icons labelled exactly "Opaque", "Aligned", "MisIndexed", their associated accent marks slate, teal, muted rust #B87550. Under these draw two separate agent-and-batch-strip icons labelled "12 batches" and "24 batches". Explicit short line underneath: "Independent sessions". Bottom small label "Same world within matched comparisons". No connecting arrow that makes 24 a continuation of 12. Do not imply all blocks have both budgets.

Panel c occupies remaining lower-right 62% width, heading "Assessment in the original session". Clear left-to-right timeline with three generously spaced document icons labelled "K1  Scientific account", "Q  Sealed predictions", "K2  Reflection". Put the stage letters on one line and the short descriptors on a second line. A thin uninterrupted rail spanning all three labelled "Full research context retained". A compact isolated side branch before K1: lock/recipe icon "Sealed recommendation" with arrow to "Independent retest"; label this branch "When required". There must be no return arrow from the retest or reference outcomes into the assessment timeline. Under Q and K2, a small lock icon and the shared short label "No reference feedback". K2 arrow goes forwards only. All labels spelled exactly, no extra paragraphs or invented numbers. Prioritize visual clarity, aligned geometry and legibility at journal page width.
