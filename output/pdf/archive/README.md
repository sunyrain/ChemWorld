# Historical PDF recovery

Current reading files are the [English complete PDF](../chemworld-ncs-en-authors.pdf),
[中文完整 PDF](../chemworld-ncs-zh-authors.pdf) and the separately retained
[ICLR edition](../chemworld-iclr2027.pdf). See the
[bilingual guide](../../../paper/venues/ncs/README.md) for Word and main-only copies.

Older NCS exports are packaged in
`archive/packages/2026-09-28/superseded-manuscripts.zip`. The
[archive guide](../../../archive/README.md) and
[original-path index](../../../archive/CONTENTS.tsv) provide recovery instructions.
This includes the earlier `ncs/` files, budget redesigns and preserved-artwork PDFs.

The [integrated review PDF](integrated-review/chemworld-integrated-review.pdf)
remains at its original path with its
[source](../../../paper/archive/integrated-review/chemworld_integrated_manuscript.md)
because the retained evidence index and builder use them. The older English
`chemworld-ncs-en-final.pdf` also remains for its legacy build-summary consumer;
it is not the current reading entry.
