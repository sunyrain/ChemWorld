### K1 interpretation

> This is the central recovery–quality tradeoff: deeper cooling increased thermodynamic recovery but also increased nucleation and approached the fines limit.

### K1 uncertainty

> The 0.007 recovery difference between batches 10 and 12 is therefore not statistically resolved by single measurements.

### Q rationale

> The heat cycle should dissolve fines and improve size distribution and purity, but its short recooling period may not restore all dissolved product.

### K2 critique

> Consequently, my sealed estimates for its surviving particles, recovery, size improvement, and reduced fines depend on an unverified assumption that heating preferentially dissolves fines without fully erasing the crystal population.

### K2 proposed

> I would repeat the batch-10 S1/C3 recipe with one deliberate change: take an HPLC measurement immediately after the 360 K reaction, then quench before seeding, cool directly to 250 K over four hours, age for four hours, filter, terminate, and perform the final assay. The reagent, solvent, catalyst, seed, stirring, cooling, aging, and filtration conditions would otherwise match batch 10.

## 24-batch session

[Retained report](../../../workstreams/flagship_tasks/reports/work-ii-c-formal-20260920-v3-auto/C-W05-B24-E-Aligned.md)

### K1 interpretation

> Therefore the major revision was not simply “cool more slowly”; it was to combine favorable upstream composition, high-temperature seeding, staged removal of supersaturation, and a final low-temperature growth/ripening hold.

### K1 uncertainty

> This interpretation is consistent with the data but seed survival or partial dissolution was not directly measured.

### K1 plateau

> Batch 23 remains the sealed recommendation, but the evidence supports a broad near-optimal plateau rather than a precisely located temperature optimum.

### Q rationale

> Q07–Q08: the 315 K temperature cycle is expected to dissolve preferentially small particles and improve size and fines, at the cost of unrecovered dissolved product after the relatively fast recool.

### K2 critique

> I mentioned this negative result but still treated thermal cycling mainly through the favorable textbook mechanism of preferential fines dissolution.

### K2 uncertainty

> Thus even the sign of its fines change relative to Q07 is uncertain.

### K2 proposed

> I would repeat the batch-23 upstream recipe—0.040 mol reagent, 0.080 L S2, 0.005 mol C1, heating toward 450 K for 900 s at 600 rpm, quenching and adding 0.050 g seed—but replace its staged cooling with one direct 14,400 s cool to 250 K followed by the same 14,400 s hold at 100 rpm, filtration and final assay. A pre-filtration particle-size measurement would be the most informative optional measurement.
