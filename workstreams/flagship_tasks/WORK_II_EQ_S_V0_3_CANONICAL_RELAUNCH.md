# Work II EQ-S v0.3 canonical relaunch

## Decision

The user authorized a complete EQ-S relaunch after identifying protocol drift in v0.2.1. The prior
run remains an immutable closed-set structural-discrimination pilot. It is not overwritten or
retrospectively relabelled.

## Participant contract

Every independent source completes twelve autonomous batches and then receives exactly the frozen
canonical v1.1 chain: K1, twelve-query Q, and K2. K1 and K2 are byte-identical to the canonical
participant prompts already used by the EQ-P v2 runner. Q uses the canonical outer prompt followed by
the preregistered EQ-S twelve-condition payload. No participant receives EQS, candidate family labels,
an equation menu, hidden truth, or a score before K2 seals.

The source goal remains mechanism characterization rather than score optimization. Opaque remains
null. Aligned and MisIndexed remain field-matched structural dossiers and therefore constitute the
registered information intervention, not a posttest answer menu.

## Why Q is retained

The twelve EQ-S conditions already passed the provider-free response-signal, scale-control,
numerical-stability, and four-parameter direct-null non-collapse checks. Changing those conditions
after observing v0.2.1 participant outputs would mix the prompt repair with an outcome-driven query
redesign. The present relaunch therefore fixes the participant-question contract while keeping the
scientifically qualified Q payload unchanged.

## Evaluation boundary

The sealed K1 report is the primary mechanism artifact. After every K2 seals and truth is released,
an external blind descriptive audit may extract proposed species, equations, evidence boundaries,
true-backbone coverage, treatment of an aqueous intermediate or alternative networks, and uncertainty
calibration. This audit is not another participant question, exposes no candidate answer during the
session, authorizes no single composite score, and does not turn post-hoc labels into a preregistered
family-accuracy endpoint.

Q is scored numerically using point error, 80% interval coverage, interval width, interval score,
response-shape error, and scale-control transfer. K2 remains a reflective audit rather than an
independent experiment.

## Denominator and order

- five physical worlds;
- Opaque, Aligned, and MisIndexed in every world;
- 15 independent source sessions;
- 12 completed source batches per session, 180 total;
- 3 sealed participant posttests per session, 45 total;
- 5 reference repeats for each of 12 Q conditions in each world, 300 total;
- provider-free gate, freeze, W01 three-arm canary, then W02--W05 with at most eight workers;
- reference truth and external audit only after all 15 K2 responses seal.

The shared integrated runtime optimization is allowed because its exact-replay and unchanged-payload
transition audit has already passed. It changes execution overhead, not physics, questions, budgets,
or scoring.
