# Work II DeepSeek C2 current-composite evaluator

## Question

Does the terminal corrected-semantics DeepSeek C2 cohort selectively repair an incorrect initial
world model, preserve that repair in an executable typed law, and translate its final recommendation
into a reproducible blind action outcome? This is a provider-free evaluation of preserved participant
trajectories, not a new participant campaign.

## Fixed units and coverage

- Participant authority: 120 unaffected cells from
  `work-ii-deepseek-c2-public-v0.2-20260814` plus the complete 15-cell A-S crystallization replacement
  from `work-ii-deepseek-c2-as-crystallization-resource-recovery-v0.2-20260815`.
- Retain 45 matched task–world clusters and all 135 assigned cells: A-E 25/75, A-P 10/30 and A-S
  10/30. Do not substitute the superseded crystallization cells or any development evaluator result.
- Execute the already registered held-out query sets once per cluster: four queries for each A-E
  cluster and 16 for each A-P/A-S cluster, for 420 truth executions in total.
- Score all five frozen checkpoints in every cell. Evaluate the final typed law summary in all 135
  cells. Schedule six paired blind executions per assigned cell; launch them only for the 121
  qualification-complete cells and retain the other 84 executions as failed/unstarted action
  denominator.
- Evaluator provider calls and participant feedback are both zero.

## Measurements

- Checkpoint normalized MAE, pre-to-final improvement and the failure-aware selective-correction
  contrast, reported separately for A-E, A-P and A-S under the frozen C2 intersection–union rule.
- Final-law schema presence, registered-query executability, normalized MAE, pre-to-law improvement,
  final-prediction compression difference and prediction-consistency error.
- Paired blind incumbent and recommendation means, gain over incumbent, exact replay, failures and
  all scheduled/launched/completed denominators.
- Participant terminal class, checkpoint scoreability and all evaluator failures. Qualification-passed
  cells are `completed`; non-qualified cells that finished their planned experiment count are
  `failed`; non-qualified cells that stopped earlier are `right_censored`.

## Pass, failure and stop rules

- Every truth or blind execution is write-once. A platform interruption may resume only missing units
  after validating every existing plan, report and receipt; no scientific result is replaced.
- A truth failure, unscorable checkpoint, non-executable law, blind failure, participant failure or
  right-censoring remains in its assigned denominator. Failed/right-censored participant cells receive
  the frozen symmetric adverse improvement bounds `[-1, 1]` for confirmatory inference.
- A-E uses the registered three-component failure-aware gate. A-P and A-S each require a positive
  failure-aware task-fixed-effect contrast and positive means in both tasks. Public C2 passes only if
  all three locus gates pass; raw effects are never pooled across nine task–locus combinations.
- Stop before publishing the analysis if a current config cannot reconstruct its registered query
  set, participant identity drifts across sources, exact replay fails, or the production evaluator
  cannot validate a stored unit.

## Expected outputs

- Ignored raw evaluator root containing the composite input binding, 45 truth packs, up to 121 blind
  packs, progress and a terminal machine summary.
- Tracked machine and Chinese reports with exact prediction, law, action, failure and censoring
  denominators. These reports may update the paper story but do not create a new release manifest,
  readiness certificate or repository-wide hash inventory.
