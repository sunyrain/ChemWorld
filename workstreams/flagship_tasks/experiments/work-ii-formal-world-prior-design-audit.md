# Work II formal world/prior design audit

Date: 2026-08-09
Claim: `Codex /root — W2-04/W2-05/W2-07-DESIGN — DOING`

## Question

Can the five frozen-law reference tasks expose matched `opaque`, `aligned_nominal` and
`misindexed_nominal` contracts with task-valid causal material fields, non-overlapping development,
public-formal and private namespaces, and resource/checkpoint contracts suitable for the H3 paired
contrast?

This block qualifies design and deterministic environment contracts only. It does not call a
provider and does not produce participant-performance evidence.

## Tested units and coverage

- Tasks: electrochemical conversion, reaction-to-crystallization, reaction-to-distillation,
  partition discovery and safety-constrained reaction.
- Prior arms: opaque identifiers, aligned nominal dossiers, and one fixed two-row dossier
  transposition per task.
- Design units: all five task contracts, all three prior arms, development seeds `[0,1,2,3,4]`,
  five preregistered public-formal world identities per task, and a separately sealed private
  namespace commitment.
- Campaign units: four participant-owned complete experiments with checkpoints at 0, 1, 2 and 4
  complete experiments, one persistent session and one shared campaign resource ledger per cell.

## Measurements

- dossier schema, field/value multiset, wording, confidence statement and canonical hash equality;
- target-field causal reachability and task-specific observable response variation;
- task, prior, world, noise, budget, safety and checkpoint matching across arms;
- split identity and non-overlap checks without exposing private world identities;
- operation, vessel, assay, instrument, stock and process-time resource ceilings;
- exact denominators and every failed task/field/world check.

## Pass and failure rules

- All five tasks must pass; a failure cannot be repaired by deleting a task or choosing a task based
  on participant outcomes.
- Aligned and misindexed dossiers must contain exactly the same property-bundle multiset and public
  wording; only the frozen anonymous-ID assignment may differ.
- The transposed field must affect an executable hidden-law response under the frozen task, while
  the evaluator-side discriminating design remains absent from the primary participant trajectory.
- Development/qualification, public-formal and private identities must be disjoint. Only the private
  commitment is tracked; private identities remain sealed.
- Every resource card must accommodate four complete lifecycles plus only its declared stage-repeat
  reserve. Any failed design check keeps W2-04 open and blocks formal execution.

## Expected outputs

- task-specific persistent-campaign configs for all five tasks;
- one machine-readable formal design/cohort manifest and deterministic audit report;
- focused tests for prior matching, resource cards, split separation and fail-closed validation;
- an updated Work II TODO only after every frozen acceptance rule passes.
