conclusions remain unchanged? This is a platform-defect recovery of the existing composite block,
not a new participant campaign or a redesigned analysis.

  and fidelity, and blind recommendation gains with the original denominators.

  and unstarted blind executions remain in their original assigned denominators.

## Failure and stop rules

- Any new truth or blind execution is write-once; the recovery root must not contain units from the
  earlier defective evaluator run.
- A platform failure, invalid stored unit or exact-replay mismatch stops the recovery. Any semantics
  change after the first recovered unit requires another full restart from unit 1.
- Do not overwrite or delete the prior evaluator root; it remains historical defect evidence.
