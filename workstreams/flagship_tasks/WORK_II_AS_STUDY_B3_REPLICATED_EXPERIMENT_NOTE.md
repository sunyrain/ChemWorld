# Work II A-S Study B3 replicated identifiable-law and novel-action experiment

Status: frozen before participant execution; independent v0.2 follow-up that leaves B2 and B3 v0.1
unchanged.

## Question and units

When the participant-visible packet makes the 1.75 partition exponent identifiable on a disjoint
scoring roster, does the participant recover the registered family and exponent, preserve accurate
unseen predictions, and choose an unexecuted action that improves on the visible evidence incumbent?
Five frozen public A-S partition worlds, three prior arms, and two independent fresh provider sessions
per arm/world yield 30 formal two-turn sessions. Replicates estimate provider stochasticity but remain
nested within five world clusters. Participant physical experiments are zero.

## Measurements

Pre/post prediction error, mechanism-family choice, exponent and absolute error, typed-law
commitment, confidence, selected scoring-query action, true action rank and gain over the evidence
incumbent, and within-world/within-replicate arm contrasts. Provider attempts, two-turn continuity,
schema failures, elapsed time, and usage are retained separately from scientific outcomes.

## Qualification and stop rules

The v0.1 frozen roster, five development seeds, five public seeds, candidate families, 0.10 exponent
tolerance, 0.02 action-opportunity threshold, and disjoint evidence/scoring denominators are unchanged.
The retained v0.1 public preflight showed that two fixed public worlds lacked an action exceeding the
evidence incumbent by 0.02 even though the structural law remained scoreable. Before any participant
call, v0.2 therefore separates the reviewer-requested structural-identification control from the
action-opportunity diagnostic: all five fixed worlds enter structural scoring; positive action gain is
scored only where the frozen opportunity criterion is met; Top-1, rank and regret are reported in all
worlds. No world is replaced. A three-session canary uses only replicate 1 of the first world and tests
schema, packet identity, continuity, typed fields, and novel action selection; its scientific answers
do not alter the design. After formal session 1, any runtime or schema change requires restarting all
30 formal sessions from unit 1. Scientific and participant schema failures are retained without
replacement; only classified infrastructure failures use the frozen bounded retry rule.

## Expected outputs

Provider-free qualification and public truth, a canary summary, 30 immutable formal cell results,
provider receipts, a machine summary separating cell, replicate-block, and world denominators, and a
Chinese interpretation for Figure 4 and the ICLR text.
