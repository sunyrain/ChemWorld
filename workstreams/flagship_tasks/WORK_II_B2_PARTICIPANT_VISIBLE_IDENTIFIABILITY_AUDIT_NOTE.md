# Work II B2 participant-visible alternative-law identifiability audit

Status: frozen before analysis; reviewer-requested audit of the completed A-S B2 block.

## Question

Does the information actually shown to an A-S B2 participant uniquely distinguish the registered
1.75-power partition law from a re-parameterized linear law or an empirical saturation/endpoint
model? This audit evaluates the evidence surface, not participant quality.

## Frozen sources and units

- The five completed public B2 worlds and their exact 8-row evidence / 8-query scoring packets.
- The B2 prompt and output schema, the executable partition kernel, and the nominal-pair coordinate
  construction. No provider call, participant rerun, private reasoning, or favorable-world selection.
- The public world is the reporting unit. Algebraic non-identifiability is reported independently of
  any fitted-model summary.

## Measurements

1. Whether the B2 phase-process roster varies the nominal solvent/extractant pair on which the
   exponent acts.
2. Whether a linear law with a free effective coefficient can reproduce the registered power law on
   the participant-visible coordinate surface.
3. Cross-validated fit of registered linear, registered 1.75-power, free-exponent power, and
   empirical constant/saturation alternatives using only participant-visible fields.
4. The positive-control sensitivity of the public-summary readout in the aligned arm.

## Pass and failure rules

- Structural-family identification is supported only if the participant-visible design has no exact
  parameter alias and the registered 1.75 family uniquely outperforms every frozen alternative on
  disjoint scoring queries in all five worlds.
- Any exact exponent/coefficient alias is a design failure for structural-family identification,
  regardless of simulator-internal paired-law gaps.
- Failure does not invalidate B2 prediction updating. It narrows B2 to numerical revision and public
  exact-law expression on the completed packet.

## Outputs

- One machine-readable audit and one Chinese report with the exact alias, fitted alternatives,
  positive-control sensitivity, and the allowed Paper 2 wording.
