- Diagnostic grid, reference context and 20 recipes are identical to the seed-0 note.
- The aligned window is centered on the highest-score grid recipe, with deterministic ties resolved

- The misspecified window is centered on the lowest-score grid recipe, with deterministic ties
  resolved by higher potential index then higher current index.
- Qualification requires 20/20 execution, 20/20 exact replay, distinct centers and aligned-minus-
  misspecified score `>= 0.10`.

  runner labels are transport-only and are not exposed to the model.

Aligned and misspecified model payloads must match fields, wording, precision, confidence and token
budget. Experimental evidence is authoritative. No scientific outcome is an operational pass rule;
all terminal outcomes and failures are retained. A persisted scientific trajectory is never replaced.
