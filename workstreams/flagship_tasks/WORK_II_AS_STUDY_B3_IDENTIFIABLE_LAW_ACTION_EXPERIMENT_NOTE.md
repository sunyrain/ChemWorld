# Work II A-S Study B3 participant-visible law identification and novel-action bridge

Status: frozen before provider-free qualification or participant execution; reviewer-requested
follow-up independent of B2.

## Question

When a fresh participant receives a packet that makes the partition exponent identifiable from
publicly supplied reference coefficients across multiple nominal identities, can it select the
registered law family, estimate the exponent, preserve accurate unseen predictions, and choose a
previously unexecuted action that improves on the visible evidence incumbent?

## Units and frozen coverage

- Participant: `deepseek-v4-flash`, high reasoning, native Codex `exec`, no tools or files.
- Five registered public A-S partition worlds; arms `opaque`, `aligned_nominal`, and
  `misindexed_nominal`; one fresh two-turn session per arm and world, 15 formal sessions.
- Provider-free qualification uses only development seeds `0..4`. It enumerates the frozen
  nominal-identity x phase-process grid under linear and 1.75-power laws, then selects one common
  8-row evidence roster and one disjoint 8-query scoring/action roster by a deterministic
  lexicographic rule. Public outcomes never change the roster.
- Every public packet includes the effective reference partition coefficient for each nominal pair,
  anonymous candidate formulas, evidence observations, and disjoint scoring/action recipes.
- Public truth for both registered laws is executed before participant calls. Failure of the frozen
  public decodability or action-opportunity preflight stops the block without redesign.

## Measurements

- Pre/post normalized absolute error on all scoring query-metric terms.
- Pre/post anonymous mechanism-family choice, estimated exponent, exponent error, and confidence.
- A typed executable power-law commitment separate from free-text summary.
- Selected unexecuted scoring/action query, its provider-free score, the visible evidence-incumbent
  score, and gain over incumbent.
- Aligned positive-control retention and misindexed structural correction by world.

## Qualification and stop rules

- The selected packet must contain at least four distinct reference coefficients and at least four
  distinct nominal pairs in both evidence and scoring rosters.
- On development qualification worlds, the 1.75-power law must beat linear and frozen empirical
  alternatives on the disjoint scoring roster, with exponent identifiable within `0.10` by the
  provider-free reference fitter in 5/5 worlds.
- The scoring/action roster must contain at least one unexecuted recipe whose true score exceeds the
  best evidence-row score by at least `0.02`, and at least two non-improving recipes, in 5/5
  qualification worlds.
- Before participant calls, all five public worlds must pass exact execution/replay, law
  decodability, exact denominators, and action-opportunity checks. A failed public preflight is a
  retained scientific/design rejection and does not authorize roster replacement.
- Canary checks only schema, two-turn continuity, packet identity, typed mechanism fields, and action
  selection. Canary scientific outcomes never enter the formal result.
- After the first formal cell, any execution-semantics or schema change requires restarting all 15
  formal sessions from unit 1. Directional outcomes never alter the design.

## 2026-08-15 development implementation correction

The first provider-free candidate-grid pass completed all `1,280/1,280` registered linear/power
truth queries and then stopped before roster freeze, public truth, canary, or any provider call. The
initial implementation had added an unregistered requirement that evidence span eight nominal
pairs; this raised the evidence incumbent enough to fail the unchanged `0.02` action-opportunity
gate in one development world. The corrected deterministic rule uses the registered minimum of four
distinct pairs: order pairs by reference coefficient and ID, take the two lowest-minimax-score rows
per pair with diagnosticity and query ID as tie-breakers, enumerate four-pair combinations in that
order, and freeze the first combination for which one common eight-query scoring roster passes the
registered gain, non-improving-action, and four-pair gates in all five development worlds. Thresholds,
worlds, candidate coverage, metrics, public rejection rules, and participant denominators are
unchanged. The completed candidate-grid truth is retained and reused. Runner liveness is emitted
every 30 seconds during long provider-free truth units.

The first exponent-grid unit then retained an `8/8` static failure because the registered
`partition_power_response_stress_v1` intervention accepts only finite exponents greater than one.
No trajectory, public outcome, or provider call was produced. Qualification therefore uses the
registered superlinear domain `1.05..2.25` in `0.10` increments, while the already executed linear
law remains the explicit exponent-1.0 alternative. The target `1.75`, recovery tolerance, roster,
worlds, and all pass/failure rules remain unchanged; the unsupported sublinear failure is retained
under the original exponent-grid output path.
