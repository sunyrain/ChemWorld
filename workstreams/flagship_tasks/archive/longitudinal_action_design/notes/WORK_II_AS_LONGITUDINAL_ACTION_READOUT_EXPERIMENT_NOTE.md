# Archived: Work II A-S longitudinal mechanism-to-action experiment

Status: design-frozen before provider-free candidate truth or participant execution. Provider
execution is not authorized by this note.

## Question

After one persistent agent autonomously completes twelve experiments and commits its final typed
mechanism snapshot, can it use only that self-acquired campaign evidence to predict, rank, and
select the best of newly revealed held-out partition operations?

## Units and frozen coverage

- Five fresh partition worlds with seeds `153150025`, `395988875`, `302275745`, `481313948`, and
  `491681886`; arms `opaque`, `aligned_nominal`, and `misindexed_nominal`; 15 persistent sessions.
- Every session controls twelve participant-chosen experiments in one unchanged thread, with typed
  belief checkpoints after 0, 3, 6, 9, and 12 completed experiments. No protocol-owned diagnostic
  experiment or matched-evidence packet is inserted.
- The final checkpoint is committed before candidate reveal. Only then are eight outcome-hidden,
  evaluator-frozen operations shown. The packet is identical across the three arms within a world.
- Candidates are selected before provider calls from a 128-operation reserved pool at registered
  hidden rank positions `1, 18, 35, 52, 69, 86, 103, 120`, with at least four nominal
  solvent-extractant pairs. They are disjoint from checkpoint queries. Any exact collision with a
  participant-executed recipe is retained and marks that cell's readout contaminated; no candidate
  is replaced.
- The participant predicts four metrics for all eight candidates, ranks every candidate exactly
  once, and selects the first-ranked query. It cannot generate a new recipe or retain an incumbent.

## Measurements

- Primary action endpoints: selected hidden rank, Top-1 accuracy, raw and normalized regret, and
  selected score minus the random-candidate mean.
- Prediction endpoint: normalized absolute error over `8 x 4 = 32` terminal candidate metrics.
- Mechanism endpoints are read only from the pre-reveal final typed checkpoint: final held-out
  prediction error and executable-law normalized MAE. The frozen adequacy threshold is `0.05`;
  this is a predictive mechanism-adequacy criterion, not a claim that one symbolic family name is
  uniquely identified. The four-way
  mechanism/action table separates correct-law/correct-action, correct-law/wrong-action,
  wrong-law/correct-action, and wrong-law/wrong-action cells.
- Arm contrasts are clustered by world. Selected score minus the best observed campaign score and
  candidate collision status are secondary diagnostics, not action pass gates.

## Qualification, failure, and stop rules

- Before participant calls, all 128 candidates in all five worlds must execute and replay exactly;
  every terminal packet must contain eight unique recipes, the registered rank positions, at least
  four nominal pairs, and no checkpoint-query collision.
- A provider canary uses one world across all three arms and checks the twelve-experiment lifecycle,
  same-thread continuity, checkpoint-before-reveal gating, complete 32-term predictions, complete
  ranking, and atomic selection only. Canary scientific outcomes are excluded.
- All 15 formal sessions and all failures are retained. After the first formal operation, changing
  rounds, candidate generation, reveal timing, schemas, metrics, or scoring requires restarting the
  full formal block. Participant outcomes never select replacement worlds or candidates.
- This experiment supports a longitudinal mechanism-to-action claim only for cells that complete
  the campaign, commit the final checkpoint before reveal, and produce an uncontaminated terminal
  readout. It does not by itself establish transfer to another task or autonomous recipe invention.

## Expected outputs

- Frozen protocol, provider-free candidate truth and action-plan bindings, per-world campaign
  configs, canary and 15 formal cell records, exact replay, terminal readout evaluations, machine
  summary, Chinese report, and Paper 2 claim updates.
