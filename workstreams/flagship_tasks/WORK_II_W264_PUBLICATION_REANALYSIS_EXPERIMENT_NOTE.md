# Work II W2-64 publication reanalysis and release-data projection

Status: frozen provider-free reanalysis; no participant or physics execution is authorized.

## Question and fixed coverage

Can the completed Work II evidence be reported with publication-safe estimands, exact denominators
and decision-aligned law diagnostics without changing any historical experiment or calling a
provider? The fixed inputs are the terminal cross-model four-condition action report, the terminal
cross-model C2 and B3 reports, and all `45` retained W2-50 task--world--prior cell records with their
frozen candidate packets, truth scores, participant rankings and final executable laws.

No historical result is overwritten or promoted from development to formal evidence. Oracle
qualification, stop rules, provider responses, participant actions, worlds, thresholds and coverage
remain unchanged. The output is a sanitized derived-data projection that contains no raw provider
payload, thread/session identifier, credential, absolute path or private seed.

## Measurements and estimands

The primary four-condition action estimand is the all-`45`-scheduled, failure-aware strategy
contrast within each model. Missing rankings, donor failures, donor-blocked recipients and recipient
failures retain normalized regret `1`. Donor-eligible results are secondary availability-conditioned
sensitivities. For autonomy minus no evidence, the sensitivities report the existing
eligible-stratum-weighted estimate plus equal-task and equal-task--world-cluster means; task-specific
means are retained.

C2 reports scheduled sessions, completed cells, prediction checkpoints, executable laws and blind
action readouts separately for each model. B3 reports completed and scheduled denominators, and
reports useful action gain both among evaluable opportunity rows and on the fixed scheduled
opportunity denominator with failed or unavailable rows counted as zero.

For each W2-50 cell with a valid final executable law, the law is evaluated on the same eight frozen
candidate ActionPlans. The derived record includes law-implied Top-1 and normalized regret,
participant Top-1 and normalized regret, whether the participant follows the law-implied Top-1,
complete-ranking, Spearman and pairwise law/action agreement, and a four-way classification:
truth-useful law versus truth-poor law crossed with followed versus not followed. A law is
decision-useful when its implied Top-1 is truth Top-1; this classification does not treat global law
MAE as a causal treatment or as sufficient scientific understanding.

## Pass, failure and interpretation rules

The reanalysis passes only if all source denominators reconcile exactly, every W2-50 derived row can
be replayed from the frozen law/query/truth contract, and primary four-condition values reproduce
the terminal report. The required primary autonomy-minus-no-evidence estimates are approximately
`-0.0913` for DeepSeek and `+0.1102` for GPT-5.6-sol; both intervals must retain their original
clustered bounds. Any mismatch stops publication integration rather than silently selecting another
population or metric.

This block may establish reporting consistency and descriptive decision alignment only. It may not
establish a provider effect, a model ranking, a causal law-to-action effect, independence of sessions
within task--world clusters, or a new confirmatory result.
