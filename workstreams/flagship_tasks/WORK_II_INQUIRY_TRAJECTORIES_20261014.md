## Question and coverage

How do recorded expectations, actual experiments, subsequent judgments and further actions
connect within autonomous research? Use all 90 sessions enumerated by W2-251's combined
`reports/research-extension-20261014/analysis.json`: 48 original plus 42 extension, EQ and C,
five worlds, Opaque/Aligned/MisIndexed, three fresh sessions. Preserve six incomplete chains.
Read all 284 recorded versions; do not treat versions as independent judgments or revisions.
Raw sources resolve through the two fixed run designs and `case_folder`, not filename recency.

## Measurements and units

- Session is the research unit; batches, notebook versions and windows are nested.
- For every version, retain its exact public-operation cursor and full public text. Link
  new final assays since the preceding version, actual actions until the next version,
  and the first subsequent committed research operation after that later version.
- A structural feedback opportunity requires a recorded version, at least one new successful
  final assay, a later version and further committed experimental action. This says nothing
  by itself about a semantic update or its causal effect; versions can restate earlier claims.
- Export actual committed actions separately from rejected attempts. Describe tested amounts,
  volumes, materials, temperature settings, measurements and exact repeated action sequences.
  No action-imputation, theory-label inference or keyword-based reasoning score.
- Retain per-world/arm/repeat values and cohort labels. Summaries of path features use session
  values, then equal-world averages; missing values are never zero. Later MisIndexed sessions
  are not a concurrent randomized information intervention.
- Select focal chains by the manuscript's existing cases, including correct and discrepant
  interpretations. Verify every quoted version and cited operation, and distinguish explicit
  prospective instruction, matching execution, subsequent observation and analyst inference.
  The selected chains cannot estimate prevalence. Independent human review stays separate.

## Acceptance and failure rules

All 90 source records must be accounted for, with exact agreement on operation counts,
successful final-assay counts, exported measurements and notebook cursors. All source failures
remain in the report. Missing/unreadable source evidence or inconsistent temporal references
stop publication of affected conclusions and are reported explicitly; no replacement runs.
Scientific outcomes may be positive, negative, ambiguous or unobserved. Interpretation quality
is not inferred from a matching action or accurate prediction. Recovery retains its known
prompt/observation denominator mismatch and is excluded from clean transfer conclusions.
