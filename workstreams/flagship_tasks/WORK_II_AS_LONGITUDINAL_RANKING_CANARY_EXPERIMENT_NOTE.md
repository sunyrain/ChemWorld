# Work II A-S twelve-round longitudinal ranking canary

Status: development-only provider canary explicitly authorized by the user. It is not formal evidence
and does not consume the five fresh worlds reserved for W2-40.

## Question

After one persistent agent autonomously completes twelve partition experiments and commits its final
typed mechanism checkpoint, can it rank and select among eight newly revealed outcome-hidden actions
without an additional per-candidate numeric prediction table?

## Units and frozen coverage

- Reuse the already provider-exposed partition world `368103785` and the existing eight B4 terminal
  candidates. The three arms are `opaque`, `aligned_nominal`, and `misindexed_nominal`: three
  persistent sessions and 36 participant-chosen physical experiments.
- Every session uses one unchanged thread, twelve autonomous experiments, and belief checkpoints at
  `0/3/6/9/12` completed experiments. No protocol-owned evidence packet or diagnostic experiment is
  inserted into the participant campaign.
- The final checkpoint must commit before candidate reveal. The terminal packet is identical across
  arms, contains no outcomes or ranks, and uses `prediction_mode=ranking_only`.
- The participant ranks all eight candidates exactly once, selects `ranking[0]`, and submits concise
  selection and mechanism-application summaries. Candidate numeric predictions and incumbent
  retention are forbidden.
- Candidate truth is reused from the retained B4 provider-free truth block. Sixteen checkpoint truth
  queries plus exact replay are produced before provider execution. Any exact candidate recipe
  collision with the autonomous campaign is retained and marks the readout contaminated; no candidate
  is replaced.

## Measurements

- Lifecycle completion, same-thread continuity, complete experiment denominator, checkpoint schedule,
  final-checkpoint-before-reveal gating, provider/resource accounting, and exact candidate collision.
- Complete participant ranking, selected hidden rank, Top-1, raw and normalized regret,
  selected-minus-random score, and the final typed-law evaluation on frozen checkpoint queries.
- Full experiment trajectory, final law, and terminal rationale are retained for qualitative diagnosis.
  No terminal prediction MAE exists in ranking-only mode.

## Pass, failure, and stop rules

- Stop after all three scheduled sessions reach retained terminal records. Scientific, schema, agent,
  provider, and platform failures remain in the denominator and are not outcome-replaced.
- Changing the twelve-round lifecycle, checkpoint timing, candidate packet, ranking-only schema, or
  failure rules after the first provider operation would require a new development block.
- This canary can test the requested longitudinal workflow and operational feasibility. One exposed
  world cannot support arm-level or paper-level scientific claims and does not authorize W2-40 formal
  preparation or execution.

## Expected outputs

Provider-free checkpoint truth and replay, one shared campaign config, three campaign records, complete
trajectories and checkpoints, terminal rankings, machine summary, and concise Chinese report.
