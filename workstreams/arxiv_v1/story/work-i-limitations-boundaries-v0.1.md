## Limitations

ChemWorld is an executable measurement apparatus for studying experimental policy in
selected virtual chemical worlds. Its typed operations, state transitions,
instruments, resource ledgers, failure semantics, identities, and replay contracts make
agent choices auditable inside the simulator. They do not establish that a generated
procedure is executable, safe, economical, or chemically successful in a physical
laboratory. Instrument outputs are bounded state-coupled synthetic or reference-tested
signals, not empirical calibration against laboratory devices or samples. Resource
receipts are simulator accounting records, not custody records for real materials,
apparatus time, waste, hazards, or monetary cost.

The live platform surface is broader than the formal evidence in this paper. Registered
tasks, operation kinds, instruments, evaluator bindings, and executable boundary
recipes establish the qualified measurement surface; they are not an equal number of
formal agent experiments. The world-fork certificate tests two single-private-component
intervention classes across a fixed qualification set. It does not establish arbitrary
world recombination, multi-component intervention validity, third-party world
authorship, or an agent's ability to infer changed rules. Adaptation under a changed
law belongs to Work II and is outside the first paper.

Known deterministic policies provide a bounded construct/discriminant-validity
positive control. Recovering their preregistered signatures, nulls, orderings, resource
patterns, and same-identity retests shows that the profile can distinguish behaviors
fixed by construction in this apparatus. It does not validate a scalar intelligence
score, demonstrate chemical competence, or establish reliability for stochastic agent
systems. Deterministic retests are reliability evidence outside the 30 primary campaign
profiles and do not double the primary sample.

The complete-system comparison is descriptive. The two systems shared the same matched
physical world, material, observation, scoring, workflow, and resource contracts, but
they also differed in model, scaffold, decision transport, retry behavior, source
identity, and run configuration. Differences in closure, assay commitment, discard,
evidence use, operations, or endpoint context therefore belong to **distinct complete
agent systems** and cannot be attributed causally to a model backend. The observed
headline—120 closed lifecycles partitioned into 84 final assays and 36 explicit
discards—separates lifecycle closure from terminal commitment; it is not a system
leaderboard and does not by itself classify discard as good, bad, efficient, or
resource-saving.

Within-system information-arm comparisons are finite matched-world descriptions.
Nominal and opaque arms alter the supplied material dossier under otherwise matched
world contracts, but five DeepSeek world pairs, ten compiled-control world pairs per
task, and the deliberately selected fresh-session worlds do not identify a general
population information effect. Known policies did not consume the dossier, so their
matched-arm equality is an interface and identity check rather than a causal null. We
do not pool tasks, systems, worlds, or authority regimes into a universal information
benefit or a general model ranking.

The discarded-state latent-terminal audit failed its frozen formal entry gate. Although
all 36 pre-discard states were reconstructable and the outcome-blind preflight covered
all registered identities, formal execution retained only 6 resolved receipts and 30
unresolved receipts after prefix, resource-state, and final-assay precondition failures.
The original on-disk sources remained unchanged and provider calls were zero, but the
original-resource-ledger gate failed. Under the preregistered missingness rule, terminal
quality remains unresolved. All latent-dependent primary point estimates and arm point
contrasts are withheld. The six resolved rows may enter only registered observed-only
diagnostics and sharp bounds; they cannot support a complete-case terminal-quality
claim. The formal run was not repaired, repeated, or replaced.

The latent bounds are finite-population support bounds, not confidence intervals. They
retain all 36 discard identities and assign unresolved scores their preregistered
support, with estimand-specific transformations for regret, decision-time incumbents,
and campaign-oracle opportunity. At the primary threshold, the false-discard fraction
can range from 0/36 to 30/36 under the registered endpoints. Such width is the intended
representation of execution uncertainty, not evidence for either endpoint. A shadow
assay was evaluator-only: it was neither chosen nor observed by the agent, did not add
an original agent experiment, and cannot establish that discard saved real resources.

Most quantitative results are descriptive finite-world evidence. Campaigns, matched
world pairs, and closed lifecycles retain their registered roles; primitive operations,
instrument events, replay traces, deterministic retests, shadow evaluations, and figure
marks are not independent samples. Where the design enumerates a complete frozen
population, counts and fractions describe that population without super-population
p-values. Where selected worlds or incomplete pairs are used, continuous contrasts,
censoring, and exact denominators remain visible. Endpoint discordance and categorical
sensitivity do not become independent hypothesis tests merely because several metrics
are displayed.

Fresh-session evidence is intentionally narrow. It revisits two selected physical
worlds and includes eight complete matched pairs plus two right-censored pairs. These
sessions expose discovery, retention, drawdown, recovery, evidence use, and terminal
retention beyond best-score summaries, but they do not estimate population variability
or isolate provider sampling effects. Right-censored cells, unstarted opportunities,
and uncontrolled provider sampling seeds remain explicit; they are not silently
converted to complete runs or zero outcomes.

Exact replay and provenance control are reproducibility mechanisms within the
executable apparatus. They bind source, environment, action, observation, noise,
resource, and evaluator identities and can detect drift or mutation. They do not mean
that a physical batch, device, or stochastic provider decision has been reproduced.
Likewise, a reference-validated component label reports closure against its declared
software/reference slice and is not a physical-validation certificate.

These boundaries define the first paper's responsibility. Work I establishes a
controlled apparatus, validates a multidimensional behavioral readout using known
policies, and demonstrates how complete-system trajectories expose terminal and process
structure omitted by endpoint-only summaries. It does not establish autonomous
physical chemistry, general model superiority, arbitrary world design, or adaptation
to changed laws. The latter questions require separate protocols, evidence, and claims
rather than extrapolation from the present figures.

## Methods interpretation firewall

The following rules apply when integrating Results, Methods, captions, abstract, or
conclusion text:

1. Use the registered analysis unit before any lower-level event count. A campaign
   profile, matched world pair, complete-system cell, lifecycle census, or frozen
   discard population may be an analysis unit; primitive operations and replays are
   not automatically replicates.
2. Distinguish apparatus qualification, deterministic positive controls, descriptive
   complete-system evidence, formal compiled controls, development trajectories, and
   fresh-session evidence. One evidence role cannot be promoted by aggregation into
   another.
3. Keep model, scaffold, transport, action authority, evidence access, retry behavior,
   and system identity separate. Use backend-only language only for an actually isolated
   backend intervention.
4. Treat null as undefined under a declared denominator, not as zero. This applies to
   no-opportunity campaign oracle values, no-measurement timing, absent endpoint
   context, zero positive denominators, pre-assay decision-time regret, and censored
   cells.
5. Preserve all frozen thresholds, inclusion rules, seeds, estimands, equality rules,
   missingness assignments, and display-entry gates. Do not select a threshold, world,
   arm, result, or figure panel because it is favorable.
6. Report the latent-terminal audit as `incomplete_full_report_required`. Do not publish
   a six-row complete-case primary summary, directional arm comparison, or resolved-only
   classification table.
7. Call resource quantities simulator ledger quantities unless a separate physical
   validation exists. Do not translate them into saved chemicals, real cost, safety,
   elapsed laboratory time, or environmental benefit.
8. Call instruments synthetic/reference-tested within their model-card scope. Do not
   imply empirical device calibration, detection performance on real samples, or
   laboratory transfer.
9. Keep Work II rule-learning and adaptation claims out of Work I. A controlled world
   fork establishes programmability of the apparatus, not agent adaptation.
10. Keep release logistics separate from scientific evidence. Packaging, identifiers,
    and upload verification establish availability and provenance; they do not change
    an estimand, rescue a failed gate, or enlarge the population.

## Compact manuscript boundary table

| Surface | Allowed conclusion | Required limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apparatus | Typed experimental choices and failures are executable and auditable in selected virtual chemical worlds | Not physical executability, safety, device calibration, or real resource custody |
| World forks | Two tested single-private-component interventions preserved the declared public contract and produced replayable divergence | Not arbitrary recombination, multi-component validity, or agent rule learning |
| Known policies | Frozen profiles recovered constructed behavioral signatures and deterministic retest identity | Not scalar intelligence, stochastic reliability, or chemical competence |
| Complete systems | Equal lifecycle closure coexisted with different assay/discard policies | Complete-system description, not backend causality or a leaderboard |
| Information arms | Fixed paired-world profiles describe the tested worlds and authority regime | Not a general population information effect |
| Latent terminal | Full failure status and preregistered sharp bounds are available | Terminal quality unresolved; no complete-case or directional result |
| Compiled controls | Outcome, prediction, calibration, and claim readouts remain separate | Not an LLM-versus-optimizer contest or composite score |
| Fresh sessions | Selected-world process profiles reveal structure omitted by endpoints | Not population variability, provider-causal attribution, or hidden censoring |
| Replay/provenance | Exact executable identity and drift checks are available | Not reproduction of a physical batch, device, or stochastic provider choice |

## Locked prohibited shortcuts

- “15 tasks evaluated” when the evidence is a 15-task registered surface.
- “29 operations” when the registered operation-kind count is 28 and
  `discard_batch` is a separate campaign terminal control.
- “independently configured models” in place of distinct complete agent systems.
- “discard saved experiments/resources” or “shadow assay was the agent's decision.”
- “six resolved latent scores show …” as a population terminal-quality statement.
- “nominal information improves performance” without the exact finite-world/system/task
  boundary.
- “exact replay reproduced the experiment” without saying executable-world replay.
- “reference validated” as a synonym for laboratory validated.
- pooled task/system/world rankings or an unregistered composite intelligence score.
- any Work II adaptation or law-learning result in the Work I title, abstract, figures,
  conclusions, or release claims.
