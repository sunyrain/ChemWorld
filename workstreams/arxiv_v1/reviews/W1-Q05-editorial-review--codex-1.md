## Editorial assessment

The manuscript has a coherent six-step argument:

1. the executable-world contract defines the controlled apparatus;
2. single-private-component forks qualify bounded programmability;
3. preregistered known policies validate the measurement profile;
4. complete systems show that lifecycle closure does not determine terminal policy;
5. compiled controls separate outcome, prediction, calibration and claim readouts; and
6. fresh trajectories expose process variation that endpoint summaries omit.

The title states the central methodological contribution without claiming physical
validation. The abstract is numerically dense but remains logically ordered, identifies
the primary analysis units, preserves the 6/36 resolved and 30/36 unresolved latent audit,
and ends with the explicit virtual-world boundary. The Introduction and Discussion repeat
the same claim ceiling, and the Conclusion returns to the process-measurement contribution
without upgrading it to a scalar ranking or a physical-laboratory result.

## Scope and prior-review closure

The current manuscript closes the language issues recorded by W1-Q04. It consistently uses
simulator-world or executable-trajectory replay language; limits the fork result to two
named single-private-component interventions; excludes arbitrary recombination, law
learning and physical transfer; and identifies operation, recipe, binding, campaign,
lifecycle and pair counts as distinct units.

The manuscript also consumes the corrected downstream outcomes of the earlier Q01--Q03
findings without erasing their history. In particular, the latent audit remains a failed
formal entry gate with 6 resolved and 30 unresolved receipts, and latent-dependent point
estimates and arm contrasts remain withheld. This Q05 assessment relies on the accepted
current L/D artifacts and the W1-D08 verification attestation; it is not a second systems
or methods audit.

## Journal fit

The strongest fit is a scientific-AI or computational-methods audience interested in
measurement, reproducibility and autonomous experimentation. The methodological novelty is
the controlled apparatus and multidimensional process profile, not a claim of chemical
discovery or model supremacy. A venue that requires wet-laboratory or sim-to-real evidence
would require a separate physical-bridge study; adding such a claim to this paper would
weaken its current evidence discipline. No journal-specific formatting decision is needed
for the arXiv v1 release.
